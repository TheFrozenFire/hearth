# Hearth

A word game for the kitchen table. The rules are the familiar ones. There are no ads and no account. The game stays on the phone.

Play it here: [https://thefrozenfire.github.io/hearth/](https://thefrozenfire.github.io/hearth/)

## Add it to an iPhone

1. Open the link in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button.
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**.

It then opens full screen, like an app. The same steps work on Android from the browser menu: **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

## How to play

You go first against Nell, or you pass one phone back and forth for two people. Easy is the gentle game. Fair makes Nell look harder for a better word.

- The first word has to cover the star.
- Tap a tile, then tap a square. You can also drag a tile onto the board.
- A blank tile asks which letter it is. Blanks score nothing.
- The score shows before you commit. **Play** places the word.
- If a word is missing from the list, **Play it anyway** keeps it for the rest of this game.
- **Suggest a move** shows one strong play. You still decide whether to use it.
- **Swap** needs at least seven tiles left in the bag, and it uses the turn.
- Six turns in a row with no points ends the game.

Leave a game and it is still there on the start screen. Clearing the browser's saved data for this site removes it.

## Words

The playable list is the public-domain ENABLE lexicon plus a short supplement of modern words and everyday Canadian and British spellings. A few slurs are left out so the computer player will not play them. Details are in [words/NOTICE.txt](words/NOTICE.txt).

Scrabble is a trademark of its owners. Hearth is an independent game and is not connected with them.

## This version

Nell plays by the rules on the phone. She is not a language model, and a turn is not sent anywhere. A later version may add an opponent that uses xAI. That opponent is not in this version.
