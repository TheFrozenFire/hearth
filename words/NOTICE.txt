Hearth word list
================

The playable list in words.txt is built from two places:

1. ENABLE (Enhanced North American Benchmark Lexicon), a public-domain
   word list compiled by Alan Beale with help from many people. Words
   longer than 15 letters are left out, because they cannot be played
   on this board. A common source for the file is:

     https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dolph/dictionary/master/enable1.txt

2. words/supplement.txt, a short list of extra words added for this
   game: common modern words (email, selfie, wifi, and so on), the
   short words qi, za, da, oi, po, and te, and everyday Canadian and
   British spellings such as colour, centre, and favourite.

A short set of slurs is removed so the computer player does not play
them. The removed words are identified by hash in tools/block.sha256.
tools/build-wordlist.py rebuilds words.txt.
